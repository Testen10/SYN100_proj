Where to Go

E-waste/ batteries/ appliances

Dream E-waste

Provides free drop-off or pick-up recycling of:

  • All e-waste products
  • Cables and wires
  • Batteries
  • Bikes
  • Some gym equipment
  • Some appliances

EnviroGreen Electronic Recycling Services

Provides drop-off or scheduled pick-up service, and most items have a small fee to recycle.
Accepts more electronic items like:

  • Medical equipment
  • Circuit Boards
  • Light bulbs
  • Older technology like VHS tapes
