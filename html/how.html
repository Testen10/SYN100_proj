How to Recycle

Categories

: Recycling works best when materials are sorted correctly.

E-waste

: phones, laptops, chargers, headphones
: Use designated electronic recycling locations. These items should never go in regular trash or recycling bins.

Batteries

: Require special handling and should be taken to approved drop-off points. Mixing batteries with trash or recycling can create fire risks

Clothes and textiles

: Can often be donated or recycled through textile programs rather than thrown away. Even worn items may be recyclable.

Large items

: mattress toppers or small furniture
Should be handled through designated programs or facilities. These items can clog dumpsters and are often recyclable through specialized services
